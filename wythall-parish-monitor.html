About this monitor

A free, transparent record of conditions across Wythall parish, built entirely on open government data. Anyone can check the sources.

Data sources

FLOOD · RAINFALL

Environment Agency

Real-time river levels and rain gauges. Open Government Licence, no key required.

CRIME · ASB

Police.uk

Street-level crime and outcomes, published monthly per police force.

WEATHER · AIR

Open-Meteo

Free forecast and air-quality API, drawing on national monitoring networks.

BOUNDARY

ONS Open Geography

Wythall civil parish boundary (E04010276), simplified for display.

How it works

Each layer pulls live from its source when the page is hosted online. Incident locations are bucketed against the parish boundary and weighted to build the heatmap. When a feed can't be reached — as in an offline preview — the page falls back to sample figures and marks the panel sample so nothing is ever presented as live when it isn't.
